## Long description (product page)

**Windows tells you how much data you used. netvis tells you who used it.**

netvis watches every connection your PC makes and breaks it down by
program, live. When something is quietly uploading in the background, you
see which app it is, how much it has moved, and where it's going — and you
can stop it with one click.

**Know what's running**

- A live graph of your whole connection, plus a table of every app using it
- Total downloaded and uploaded per program, not just a number for the PC
- Open a program's details to see each connection it holds: remote address,
  port, protocol, and the hostname behind the IP

**Stop what you don't want**

- Cut any program off the internet instantly, without touching Windows
  Firewall rules
- Or leave it online and cap it: separate download and upload speed limits,
  permanently or for a set number of minutes, hours or days
- Auto-block: set a threshold and netvis cuts off anything that goes over it
  on its own

**Block ads, trackers and malicious domains**

- Blocking happens at the DNS level, for the whole computer — every browser,
  and every app that isn't a browser
- Ships with a large built-in blocklist, and handles apps that try to slip
  past it using DNS-over-HTTPS or DNS-over-TLS
- Add your own domains, or import a list from a text file

**Notice things you'd otherwise miss**

- Alerts when a program connects out for the first time, starts accepting
  incoming connections, or your DNS servers change
- Windows notifications, so you're told even when netvis is in the tray

**Stays out of the way**

- Runs in the tray and keeps monitoring, blocking and limiting with the
  window closed
- Starts with Windows if you want it to
- One small native app. No account, no cloud, no telemetry — your traffic
  data never leaves your computer

## What you get

A license key for one computer, valid for 6 months. Windows 10 or 11,
64-bit. Requires Administrator to run, because inspecting and filtering
traffic needs driver-level access.

## Honest limits

netvis is not an antivirus and not a VPN. It shows you what your programs
are doing on the network and lets you stop them — that's the job.
